# Interactive Forms

**Forms are responsive and built with conditional logic** — *meaning that the questions and/or options presented to you may change depending on how you answer other questions in the form. This is to try to keep the questions relevant to how you wish to interact.*

> **Each form is collapsed under the "Form - Title >" bars** — just click on the bar and it will reveal the form. It's similar to the "Keep Reading/Read More" cuts on tumblr.

> [!warning] Direct Form Links Available
> 
> If you'd rather fill out the form directly on the service's site, [[Interactive Forms#Direct Form Links\|links are provided at the bottom of this page]].
>

# Disinterest Tracker

> [!rule]- Interest Assumed For All Open Muses Unless Filled Out
> Unless you fill out the **Disinterest Tracker** to tell me otherwise, if we're mutuals, it's assumed you're interested in interacting with any and *all* of my [[! Open Muses - Roster\|Open Muses]] in any of their canon-based verses and/or their fandom matched verses & AUs.

> [!statsbox]+ Disinterest Tracker
> 
> **What This Form Is For**
> 
> For specifying which of my muses and/or their respective verses you are *not* interested in, so that I will not send you things from those, otherwise any and all muses on the [[! Open Muses - Roster\|Open Muses]] roster are free game for me to send your way, though I will always match fandom to fandom unless we've discussed it and I have your explicit permission to do otherwise for crossover interactions.
> 
> **Should You Fill This Form Out?**
>

> |---|---|
> | Have Not Read Guidelines | No - *Read Those First!* |
> | Want Any & All Muses & Verses | No |
> | Not Interested In Any Muse(s) | Yes |
> | Not Interested In Any Verse(s) | Yes |
