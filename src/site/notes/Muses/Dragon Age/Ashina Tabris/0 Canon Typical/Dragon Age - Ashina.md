# Quick Info

## Summary

Ashina de Riva is the child of an Antivan Crow of House Arainai, who later becomes a Crow herself in order to gain protection from the Order of the Grey Wardens and escape a life she never wanted thanks to being conscripted by the Warden-Commander of Ferelden, only to end up the *de facto* Commander herself during the course of the Fifth Blight.

Ashina is conscripted against her will into the Grey Wardens at the start of the Fifth Blight in 9:30 Dragon, going by the name of "**Tathas Arainai**". By 9:31 Dragon, she is notorious among the Wardens for having survived killing the archdemon and ending the Blight, only then to encounter the darkspawn magister known only as the Architect several months later, and letting it live. The First Warden issued orders for her to be brought in — *alive* — to Weisshaupt for questioning. No warden has survived the attempt until she is said to have been killed by the Antivan Crows in 9:33 Dragon.

In 9:33 Dragon, Ashina enters negotiations with the Fifth Talon of House de Riva, seeking protection from the Grey Wardens in exchange for her service to the House and the Crows. She is now a Master Assassin of House de Riva, and may serve as the Fifth Talon's Second.

# Background

## Birth & Early Childhood 

Born as Ashina Tabris on the 30th of Harvestmere, 9:06 Dragon, in the Denerim Alienage in Ferelden, she is the only child of Cyrion Tabris and Adaia Tabris née Arainai.

Ashina is trained by Adaia from an early age in the skills of a rogue, much to Cyrion's chagrin, *especially* when he learns that his wife has been teaching their child about poisons and thievery as much as she has the martial skills of a rogue — though even Cyrion is unaware of just how brutal some of the training Ashina is put through truly is, as neither she nor Adaia speak of it, and Ashina does not know any better to question it.
## Childhood & Adolescence 

Adaia essentially begins teaching Ashina in much the same ways as any Crow fledgling, albeit without the torture that House Arainai is often known for — she hopes to *protect* her child from the Crows, should they ever find them, and so she does the only thing she knows in order to do so, and that is to train her child as much as a Crow as she can to handle fighting Crows in the future in order to survive if necessary.

In 9:24 Dragon, at the age of 17, Ashina loses her mother when humans kill Adaia in the streets of the Denerim Market just outside the Alienage gates. Little does she know that these humans were, in fact, Crows, who had been hunting down Adaia for the last seventeen years after she had fled House Arainai, the Crows, and Antiva.
## Adulthood 
### 9:30 Dragon
#### The Death of Ashina Tabris
In 9:30 Dragon, at the age of 24, Ashina is faced with an arranged marriage that she does not want, having avoided such for the last several years with time to grieve for her mother's loss, only for the Alienage's *hahren* to finally set an ultimatum with Cyrion that if *he* didn't arrange a match for Ashina, then Valendrian would.
